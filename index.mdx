## What is Maia?

Maia is an **AI executive platform** — build and customize AI executives that understand operations end-to-end, collaborate with teams, and run complex workflows across your company. Your executives plan, coordinate, and execute sustained operational work autonomously.

<Note>Looking for your workspace? Visit [maia.is](https://maia.is)! Also available on [iOS and Android](https://maia.is) — manage your executives from anywhere.</Note>
